DeepSeek-V4-Flash delivers top-tier intelligence at record-low cost

News time: 2026-08-01T04:33:02.137151+00:00 · Source: rss

DeepSeek has released DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, a new large language model that redefines the trade-off between intelligence, scale, and cost. Though labeled as a 304-billion-parameter model in marketing materials, technical documentation confirms it is a 284-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture — with only ~13 billion parameters activated per token — supporting a massive one-million-token context window. What sets it apart isn’t raw size, but a sharp post-training refinement focused explicitly on agentic behavior: autonomous planning, multi-step reasoning, tool use, and dynamic decision-making.

Third-party benchmarking by Artificial Analysis shows DeepSeek-V4-Flash outperforms significantly larger models like MiniMax M3 (428B) on its Intelligence Index — a composite score derived from nine rigorous evaluations including SciCode, GPQA Diamond, Humanity’s Last Exam, and AA-Omniscience. Crucially, it achieves this while costing just $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.27 per million output tokens — roughly one-tenth the cost of comparable-performing models such as Kimi K3 or GLM-5.1. On Artificial Analysis’ scatter plot of intelligence versus cost per task, DeepSeek-V4-Flash sits alone in the upper-left ‘most attractive quadrant’, far ahead of the Pareto frontier.

Real-world usage reveals another layer: its agentic capabilities are tunable. When tested on a whimsical prompt — 'a pelican riding a bicycle' — the default reasoning level produced an incoherent, malformed image description. But switching to high reasoning effort yielded a coherent, visually consistent scene: a pelican gripping handlebars, pedaling, and even holding a fish in its beak pouch — all rendered with correct proportions and motion cues. This demonstrates not just stronger multimodal grounding, but responsive, controllable agentic behavior.

The release marks DeepSeek’s strategic pivot toward efficiency-first AI. Rather than chasing parameter count records, V4-Flash prioritizes real-world utility: fast coding assistance, reliable tool calling, and robust long-context reasoning — all while running efficiently on commodity infrastructure. Its public-beta launch via Hugging Face and OpenRouter makes it immediately accessible to developers building agent-based applications, from automated research assistants to self-operating DevOps tools.
