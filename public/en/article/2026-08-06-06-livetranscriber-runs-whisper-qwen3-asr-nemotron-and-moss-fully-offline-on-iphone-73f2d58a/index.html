LiveTranscriber runs Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, Nemotron, and MOSS fully offline on iPhone

News time: 2026-08-06T04:28:18.364219+00:00 · Source: reddit

A new open-source iOS app called LiveTranscriber has demonstrated that cutting-edge speech and language models—including Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, NVIDIA’s Nemotron Streaming, and MOSS Multi-Speaker—can run entirely offline on consumer iPhones. Developed over the past month by an independent engineer, the app transforms these powerful open models into a practical, real-world tool: it delivers real-time multilingual transcription, speaker-aware diarization, on-device summarization, and even Apple Watch–enabled recording with automatic sync—all without touching the cloud.

Unlike academic demos or desktop-only prototypes, LiveTranscriber tackles the gritty realities of mobile AI: memory constraints, streaming latency, battery efficiency, and seamless model switching. The developer optimized each model for Core ML and iOS hardware—quantizing weights, managing audio chunking and context caching, and building custom inference pipelines that avoid redundant computation. For example, Nemotron Streaming’s cache-aware FastConformer-RNNT architecture is adapted to reuse encoder state across audio frames, while MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize handles multi-speaker separation and timestamp alignment in a single forward pass.

The app supports five key capabilities out of the box: fully offline ASR across 52 languages (via Qwen3-ASR), low-latency live transcription (Nemotron), speaker-differentiated transcripts (MOSS), local LLM analysis (Qwen3 for summaries and key points), and searchable transcript history. Users can download and swap models on demand—no internet required after initial setup. It’s already available on the App Store and its full source code is hosted on GitHub, inviting scrutiny and collaboration from the on-device AI community.

This isn’t just about porting models—it’s about rethinking what ‘practical’ means for edge AI. By solving concrete engineering bottlenecks—like loading a 1.2 GB MOSS model without crashing iOS memory limits or sustaining sub-500ms end-to-end latency during continuous dictation—the project sets a new benchmark for mobile-first, privacy-first AI applications. As more developers adopt similar approaches, we may see a shift away from cloud-dependent voice assistants toward truly personal, always-available, and fully controllable on-device intelligence.
