DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 launches with strong real-world engineering performance

News time: 2026-08-13T03:32:13.942375+00:00 · Source: hackernews

DeepSeek has quietly rolled out DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 — its latest flagship large language model — now live on the official API endpoint as of August 12, 2026. Unlike previous releases, there is no dedicated announcement page, blog post, or benchmark report from DeepSeek itself. Instead, users first learned of the update via OpenRouter’s model listing and scattered social media reposts, including a WeChat-originated post shared by ChrisGPT on X. The model is available exclusively through API access, with no public documentation or official technical specs published by DeepSeek at time of writing.

Early adopters are already reporting tangible gains in production-grade workloads. One developer described running it continuously on a distributed physics engine and traffic simulator, spending roughly $12.50 for 2 billion tokens with 50% cache hit rate — and noted significant performance improvements without introducing new bugs or instability. Another user praised the prior Flash update for light tasks but said V4 Pro 0813 appears capable of heavy-duty software development at unusually low cost — calling it 'peanuts' compared to alternatives. This echoes a broader sentiment among practitioners: intelligence isn’t always the bottleneck — reliability, speed, and token efficiency are.

Pricing data confirms the cost-efficiency claim: at $0.435 per million input tokens and $0.87 per million output tokens, V4 Pro 0813 undercuts many top-tier closed models while offering a massive 1,048,576-token context window and up to 384,000 tokens of output — far exceeding most competitors. Yet the lack of transparency remains a sore point. Commenters on Hacker News criticized the reliance on third-party platforms like OpenRouter for basic discovery, urging links to official API docs or benchmark results instead.

The release also raises questions about DeepSeek’s open-weight strategy. While April’s and July’s V4 Pro checkpoints were openly released on Hugging Face, it’s unclear whether 0813 will follow suit — DeepSeek has not confirmed plans. Meanwhile, users continue to compare it pragmatically against alternatives like Kimi-K3, GLM-5.2, and Anthropic’s Sonnet and Opus — not on raw benchmarks, but on whether it gets their specific job done at the lowest sustainable cost.
