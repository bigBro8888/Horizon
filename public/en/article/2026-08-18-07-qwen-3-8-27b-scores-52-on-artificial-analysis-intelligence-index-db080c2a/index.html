Qwen 3.8 27B scores 52 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

News time: 2026-08-18T02:20:11.395416+00:00 · Source: rss

Qwen 3.8 27B — a newly released large language model from Alibaba’s Tongyi Lab — has achieved a score of 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1.1, a rigorous, multi-dimensional benchmark that evaluates real-world reasoning, agentic behavior, long-context understanding, and domain-specific capabilities like banking, scientific coding, and high-stakes exam performance. This places it on par with GPT-5.6 Luna (also scoring 52), and just one point behind GLM-5.2 (53) and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (53), despite those models being vastly larger: GLM-5.2 has 753 billion parameters, and DeepSeek V4 Pro is 1.6 billion — both orders of magnitude bigger than Qwen’s 27.8 billion.

What makes this result remarkable is not raw scale but efficiency. The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index deliberately weights performance per parameter, penalizing bloated architectures. Qwen 3.8 27B uses a dense transformer architecture — no mixture-of-experts sparsity — meaning all 27.8 billion parameters are actively engaged during inference. It retains the hybrid attention design from Qwen 3.5 and 3.6: alternating full-attention and Gated DeltaNet linear-attention blocks, supporting up to 262,144 tokens context length and multi-token prediction. Unlike many recent 20B+ models optimized for cheap training, Qwen 3.8 appears fine-tuned for robust, general-purpose intelligence across complex, real-world tasks.

The benchmark itself reflects a maturing standard in LLM evaluation. No longer limited to narrow QA accuracy, the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index now integrates terminal interaction (Terminal-Bench), financial reasoning (τ³-Banking), scientific code generation (SciCode), and even existential reasoning challenges like Humanity’s Last Exam and GPQA Diamond. Scoring highly across this suite signals broad competence — not just memorization or prompt engineering tricks. Simon Willison, a respected developer and AI commentator, called the model “truly astonishing,” underscoring how rare it is for a sub-30B model to match flagship closed-weight competitors on such a holistic metric.

For developers and enterprises weighing cost versus capability, Qwen 3.8 27B offers a compelling middle ground: it fits on consumer-grade GPUs like the RTX 6000 Ada (with quantization), enables local deployment, and avoids API latency or vendor lock-in — all while delivering near-state-of-the-art reasoning fidelity. Its success also highlights China’s accelerating pace in open, high-performance LLM development — especially as Qwen models remain fully open-weight and commercially permissive under the Tongyi License.
