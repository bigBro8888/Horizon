TabPFN-3.5 launches as new SOTA tabular foundation model

News time: 2026-09-16T05:58:30.506827+00:00 · Source: reddit

Prior Labs has unveiled TabPFN-3.5, the latest iteration of its transformer-based foundation model designed specifically for tabular data. Unlike general-purpose LLMs, TabPFN is built from the ground up to handle structured datasets — think spreadsheets, databases, or clinical records — with strong performance even on small or medium-sized tasks. But TabPFN-3.5 pushes far beyond those origins: it now dominates both major living benchmarks for tabular machine learning — TabArena and BeyondArena — especially where prior models struggled most: on datasets with over one million rows, up to 20,000 features, high categorical cardinality, and embedded text fields.

What makes this release particularly practical is its family of variants. TabPFN-3.5-Fast (currently in alpha) delivers a sixfold speedup over the base model — ideal for rapid prototyping or latency-sensitive applications. TabPFN-3.5-Thinking trades compute for accuracy, offering measurable Elo gains (+20 on BeyondArena, +44 on TabArena) via API access. And TabPFN-3.5-Plus serves as the balanced flagship version, achieving a +250 Elo lead over the strongest previous baseline on BeyondArena and +150 over the prior overall leader. These aren’t marginal improvements: in benchmark terms, an Elo gain of 250 reflects a substantial leap in real-world predictive reliability.

The significance lies not just in raw scores but in scope expansion. Earlier versions of TabPFN were optimized for smaller tables — up to 1,000 × 20,000 (rows × features), with trade-offs between dimensionality and scale. TabPFN-3.5 breaks those constraints, supporting massive, heterogeneous tables while preserving the model’s signature zero-shot inference capability and minimal hyperparameter tuning. This bridges a longstanding gap between traditional tree-based models (like XGBoost) and deep learning approaches — offering neural scalability without sacrificing usability.

For practitioners, this means faster iteration on complex business, scientific, or healthcare datasets that mix numbers, categories, and free-text fields — all without needing custom feature engineering or ensemble pipelines. For researchers, TabPFN-3.5 sets a new reference point for what’s possible in tabular foundation modeling, reinforcing the viability of transformer architectures in structured domains. As TabArena and BeyondArena continue evolving as ‘living’ benchmarks — integrating new datasets and task types — TabPFN-3.5’s leadership across IID, temporal, and grouped settings signals robust generalization, not just leaderboard gaming.
