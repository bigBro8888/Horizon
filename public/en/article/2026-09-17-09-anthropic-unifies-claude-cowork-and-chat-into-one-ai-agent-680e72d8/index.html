Anthropic unifies Claude Cowork and chat into one AI agent

News time: 2026-09-17T06:07:10.782936+00:00 · Source: rss

Anthropic has officially merged its two previously distinct AI experiences—Claude Cowork and the standard Claude chat interface—into a single, unified product simply called 'Claude'. The change, announced in a September 2026 blog post, aims to eliminate user confusion around overlapping capabilities and fragmented interfaces. As Simon Willison noted in his analysis, many users—including developers and knowledge workers—had struggled to distinguish when to use 'Claude' versus 'Claude Cowork', especially as both offered overlapping features like file access, task automation, and asynchronous follow-up.

This consolidation reflects a broader industry pivot toward general-purpose AI agents: systems that don’t just answer questions but proactively manage workflows across devices and contexts. Unlike the earlier split—where Claude Chat served as a conversational interface and Claude Cowork acted as a desktop-integrated agent for non-programmers—the new unified Claude handles both roles seamlessly. It can draft emails, reorganize folders on macOS, generate spreadsheets from screenshots, and continue working on tasks even after the user closes their laptop—blurring the line between chatbot and autonomous assistant.

The rollout begins with Pro and Max plan subscribers across web, desktop, and mobile apps, with gradual availability over the coming weeks. Notably, Anthropic is not retiring Cowork’s functionality; instead, it’s folding those capabilities—including deep file system access and background task execution—into the core Claude experience. Pricing remains tied to plan tiers, though the company clarifies that Cowork-heavy usage consumes rate limits faster than standard chat, suggesting underlying resource allocation differences persist beneath the unified UI.

This move echoes OpenAI’s recent rebranding of its Codex desktop app to ChatGPT, signaling a maturing phase in AI product design: away from siloed tools and toward cohesive, cross-context agents. For users, the benefit is simplicity; for Anthropic, it’s strategic focus—streamlining development, reducing support fragmentation, and reinforcing Claude’s identity as an agentic OS layer rather than just another LLM chat interface.
