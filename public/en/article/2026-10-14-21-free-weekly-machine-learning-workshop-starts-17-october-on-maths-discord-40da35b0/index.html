Free weekly machine learning workshop starts 17 October on Maths Discord

News time: 2026-10-14T06:06:19.823756+00:00 · Source: reddit

A new free, community-driven machine learning workshop is launching on Saturday, 17 October, hosted on the popular 'maths' Discord server (discord.gg/maths). Designed for beginners and self-learners, the workshop combines live lectures with hands-on coding labs — all conducted in a focused, math-oriented environment known for its supportive and technically engaged members.

The curriculum unfolds in three progressive parts. Part 1 covers foundational traditional ML: linear models, gradient descent as an optimization method, supervised vs unsupervised learning paradigms, and practical best practices. Part 2 introduces deep learning concepts including multi-layer perceptrons, backpropagation, autoencoders, RNNs for sequences, CNNs for images, and modern attention mechanisms and transformers — plus a brief foray into signal processing. Part 3 dives into graph machine learning, covering graph representations, shallow embeddings, knowledge graphs, graph neural networks (GNNs), and subgraph sampling — with room to expand as the series evolves.

Each session runs weekly on Saturdays from 3 to 5 PM CEST, beginning with a code-heavy first lab where instructors will review participants’ Python implementations in real time. The workshop assumes basic Python proficiency — especially with NumPy and Matplotlib — and recommends familiarity with Calculus I (and optionally Calculus II and linear algebra). While no formal credentials or institutional backing are highlighted, the emphasis is on accessibility: clear prerequisites, curated learning resources, and direct access to instructors via the Discord server.

What sets this initiative apart isn’t novelty or cutting-edge research, but structure and community scaffolding. Unlike fragmented YouTube tutorials or dense MOOCs, it offers consistent weekly rhythm, peer interaction, and immediate feedback — a rare combination for free, open-access ML education. For learners who thrive with accountability and human connection, this fills a quiet but meaningful gap in the self-paced learning landscape.
