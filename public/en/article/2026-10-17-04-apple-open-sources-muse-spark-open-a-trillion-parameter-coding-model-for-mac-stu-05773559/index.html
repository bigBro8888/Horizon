Apple open-sources Muse Spark Open, a trillion-parameter coding model for Mac Studio

News time: 2026-10-17T05:33:27.819470+00:00 · Source: hackernews

Apple ML Research has unveiled Muse Spark Open — a new open-weight large language model optimized for coding and agentic workflows — and demonstrated its efficient distributed inference across consumer-grade Mac Studios using the updated MLX framework. Unlike typical frontier models that demand datacenter-scale infrastructure, Muse Spark Open runs natively on Apple silicon, scaling from a single Mac Studio with up to 512 GB of unified memory to clusters of four units. In benchmark tests, such a cluster achieves over 36 tokens per second on 8,192-token contexts — faster than average human reading speed — marking a rare instance of near-frontier AI performance on desktop hardware.

The model is not a dense trillion-parameter beast but a highly sparse architecture: it contains 816 billion total parameters, with only 42 billion active per forward pass. Trained by Meta Superintelligence Labs and released under the Muse Model License, it emphasizes long-horizon reasoning, tool calling, and deep codebase understanding — especially across real-world repositories developers use daily. Its 1-million-token context window further supports complex software engineering tasks, far exceeding standard LLM limits.

Crucially, this deployment relies on MLX — Apple’s open-source array framework designed specifically for Apple silicon. The newly updated version adds robust support for distributed inference across multiple Mac Studios via shared memory and low-latency interconnects. MLX’s Swift, C++, and C bindings allow researchers and engineers to fine-tune, quantize, and scale models without leaving the Apple ecosystem — a notable departure from CUDA-dependent workflows.

Community response has been deeply technical and critically engaged. Commenters questioned benchmark fairness — notably comparing Meta’s model at aggressive 4-bit quantization against Qwen in native BF16 — while others reported running competitive models like DeepSeek-V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3 Flash on M5 Ultra/Max with strong throughput and quality retention. Some users pointed out that the 8K-context benchmark, though fast, falls short of Muse Spark Open’s full 1M-token capability — suggesting real-world utility may hinge on better context-aware optimization. Ultimately, Apple hasn’t just shipped a model; it’s showcased a viable, scalable stack for frontier AI on prosumer hardware.
