TempoPFN enables zero-shot time series forecasting with synthetic pre-training

News time: 2026-10-19T06:08:52.621114+00:00 · Source: reddit

TempoPFN is a new time series foundation model that achieves zero-shot forecasting—meaning it can predict unseen real-world time series without any fine-tuning or exposure to real data during training. Developed by researchers from AutoML.org and accepted at NeurIPS 2026, TempoPFN relies entirely on synthetic time series for pre-training: roughly 10 million sequences, each up to 2048 steps long, generated using a curated library of diverse, interpretable synthetic generators and augmentations. This approach deliberately avoids real-world benchmarks during training—a strict design choice that makes its strong downstream performance especially notable.

The architecture at its core is a DeltaProduct-based linear RNN. DeltaProduct is a recently introduced family of efficient linear recurrent models known for expressive state tracking and superior length extrapolation compared to predecessors like DeltaNet. Unlike traditional deep RNNs or Transformers, linear RNNs scale efficiently with sequence length and avoid attention’s quadratic cost—making them well-suited for long-horizon forecasting tasks where computational efficiency matters.

What sets TempoPFN apart is not just the model, but the data pipeline. Its synthetic generators cover patterns like trends, seasonality, changepoints, autoregressive dynamics, and stochastic volatility—designed to span the structural diversity found in real-world time series. Crucially, this same synthetic corpus has already powered prior influential methods including Toto-2 and TS-ICL, suggesting broad utility beyond a single model. The framework is fully open-source, with implementations on GitHub, pretrained models on Hugging Face, and detailed documentation on arXiv.

On the Gift-Eval benchmark—a standardized time series evaluation suite—TempoPFN matches or exceeds the performance of models trained directly on real data, despite never seeing a single real time series before evaluation. This challenges the long-held assumption that large-scale real-world data is indispensable for foundation models in time series. While zero-shot capability doesn’t yet replace task-specific fine-tuning in all production settings, it dramatically lowers the barrier to entry for forecasting on niche or privacy-sensitive domains where real data is scarce or inaccessible.
