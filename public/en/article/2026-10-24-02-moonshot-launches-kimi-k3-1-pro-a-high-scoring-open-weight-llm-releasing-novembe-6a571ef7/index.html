Moonshot launches Kimi K3.1 Pro, a high-scoring open-weight LLM releasing November 4

News time: 2026-10-24T05:46:59.324697+00:00 · Source: rss

Moonshot AI has officially launched Kimi K3.1 Pro — a new large language model that immediately stands out for its rare combination of top-tier benchmark performance, aggressive API pricing, and a firm commitment to open weights. Available now via the Kimi API, web interface, Kimi Work, and Kimi Code, the model is already live for developers and enterprises — though its full parameter weights won’t be publicly released until November 4, 2026. This makes Kimi K3.1 Pro the first near-state-of-the-art model scheduled for open release while matching or exceeding the capabilities of closed frontier models like GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon.

What sets Kimi K3.1 Pro apart isn’t just raw power — it’s strategic design. Built as a post-trained evolution of the already-impressive Kimi K3 (a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model released in July 2026), K3.1 Pro leverages substantially larger post-training compute and optimizations across the entire stack: reinforcement learning frameworks, long-context inference, rollout scheduling, and sandboxed environment quality checks. Moonshot also introduced a 'Highspeed' variant — 2–3× faster on average, priced at double the standard rate except for cached input tokens, which retain the base price.

Pricing is unusually competitive: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — identical to GPT-6.1 Sol and half the cost of Claude Opus 5.5. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 55 — ahead of Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Argon (all at 53), and only trailing Opus 5.5 (58). Notably, this is the highest score yet achieved by any model slated for open release — a milestone for transparency and reproducibility in the LLM ecosystem.

Early hands-on testing confirms strong multimodal reasoning. Developer Simon Willison used the model via the LLM CLI tool to generate SVGs of a pelican riding a bicycle — a whimsical but revealing stress test for spatial logic and instruction fidelity. Across low, medium, and max reasoning modes, K3.1 Pro consistently produced structurally sound SVGs with correct anatomy and composition, outperforming its predecessor Kimi K3 on the same task. While not yet fully open, the model’s imminent weight release signals a deliberate shift toward open intelligence — one where high capability and openness coexist without compromise.
